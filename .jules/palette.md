## 2024-05-08 - [Focus States on Icon-Only Buttons]
**Learning:** Focus states on icon-only interactive elements are frequently missed. Without visible focus indicators and explicit aria-labels, keyboard navigation and screen reader usage are severely compromised.
**Action:** Ensure all icon-only buttons have proper aria-labels and visible focus states (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-slate-900 focus-visible:ring-offset-2`).
