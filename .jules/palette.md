## 2025-06-25 - ARIA attributes for toggle buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only toggle controls like `ThemeToggle` or `HeaderMobileMenu` need `aria-expanded` or `aria-pressed` along with `aria-label` to effectively communicate both their function and current toggled state to screen readers.
**Action:** Always ensure that toggle buttons have descriptive `aria-label` and either `aria-expanded` or `aria-pressed`.
